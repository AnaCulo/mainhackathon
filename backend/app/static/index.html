Reporter

This is an AI reporting assistant. Use only made-up names and events. You can type your report, or hold the talk button. If you talk, your voice is turned into text on this device and the recording is deleted straight away — no audio leaves this device. Names are replaced before anything reaches an AI model. An authorised human case handler reviews your report and decides what happens next. You do not have to give your name.
Hold the button (or the space bar) while speaking, in full sentences. Your words appear below to check and correct before sending. The microphone is only on while Talk is selected.

Behind the scenes

Each message you send appears here step by step: local screening, what was sent to BRANE, BRANE's decision, the model check, and the case handed to a human.
