## Submission text

A worker who spots a discrepancy needs a safe way to report it and a person who will follow up. Čulo prepares a protected case for an independent handler at a Dutch employer. The handler sees the reported facts, timeline and evidence, chooses whether to investigate, close or escalate, and separately approves feedback. The workflow records actual acknowledgement and delivery results and tracks both statutory clocks.

Our synthetic demonstration follows a single ledger discrepancy from text intake to a published n8n webhook, durable register, local reporter mailbox, designated handler email capture and human approval form. Eight concurrent first deliveries created one case. In two timed local runs, the handler could open the checked brief in 86 and 143 milliseconds. The check and model in those runs were fixtures. The protected return route and email delivery were local captures.

The team’s live policy-v1 baseline exposed failures on protected details, repeated identifiers, embedded instructions and special-category data. The submission preserves those observations. A separate disposable backend outage returned 503 with zero additional model or webhook calls. The live configured-policy run, final team review and manual timing comparison are pending.
