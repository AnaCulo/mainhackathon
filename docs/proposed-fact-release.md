# Proposed changes for Person A’s review

The current synthetic intake rules release ordinary, independently meaningful process observations under the existing policy matrix. Review these proposals before widening the release vocabulary. No proposal below enables a transfer or changes the hosted tenant.

| Information | Current treatment | Decision needed before any expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Financial discrepancy amount and currency | Local encrypted evidence and, for recognised clauses, a local structured amount including currency and any “approximate” or “total” qualifier | Which purposes and destinations require a monetary value? Would a band suffice? Are the amount, period or supplier combination confidential? |
| Invoice counts combined with a financial reporting period | Withhold both details from the shared draft. A supported independent discrepancy and evidence availability may leave | Define limits, combinations and independent verification conditions. Decide separately for the hosted gateway/model and case workflow |
| Exact dates, shift times, roles, sites, transaction references and filenames | Excluded from the detailed transfer grammar. Identifying combinations can hold the whole report | Define an approved generalisation and a residual identification test before adding fields |
| Person-linked offence allegations, family relationships and special-category information | Whole-operation local hold on recognised high-risk material. Preserve the lawful original for authorised review | Establish purpose, local processing conditions and operator access. A pseudonym alone does not authorise release |
| Voice or production use with real people | Existing synthetic boundary applies | Complete processor, microphone, retention, access, correction, deletion and human-route reviews |

Use the team's legal analysis and matrix as the basis for sign-off. Review the combined package and its destinations on realistic synthetic narratives, including the effects of reusing small counts and relative periods in a small workplace.
